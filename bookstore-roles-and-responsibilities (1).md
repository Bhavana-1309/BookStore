# BookStore — Roles and Responsibilities

## 1. User

**Registration** – Create an account by providing essential details such as name, email, and password. The system validates the email format and enforces a secure password before the account is created.

**Profile Management** – Update personal information like name, email, and password. Users can revisit their profile anytime to keep their details current or reset a forgotten password.

**Book Browsing** – Explore available books, browse genres, and search for specific titles or authors. Users can filter results by category, price range, or rating, and sort listings to quickly find what they're looking for.

**Book Purchase** – Add desired books to the cart, choose quantities, and complete the checkout process. Once payment is confirmed, an order is created and the user receives order details for reference.

**Order Tracking** – View the status of past and current orders (e.g., Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered) and check order history at any time.

**Logout** – Securely log out from the BookStore app, ending the authenticated session.

## 2. Seller

**Registration** – Create a seller account by providing details such as business name, email, and password, distinguishing it from a regular customer account.

**Profile Management** – Update business information like name, email, and password to keep the seller's storefront details accurate.

**Book Listing** – Add new books with complete details (title, author, genre, description, price, quantity), making them visible to users browsing the platform.

**Inventory Update** – Modify stock quantity, price, or book details as availability changes, ensuring the catalog stays accurate.

**Order Fulfillment** – Process and ship orders placed by users in a timely manner, updating the order status as it moves through fulfillment.

**Logout** – Securely log out from the BookStore app.

## 3. Admin

**System Management** – Oversee all system functionalities, configurations, and security, ensuring the platform runs smoothly for all roles.

**User Management** – Create, update, or delete user accounts and manage user ratings to maintain a trustworthy customer base.

**Book Management** – Add, update, or remove book listings from the platform, acting as the final authority over the catalog.

**Seller Management** – Approve new seller accounts, update seller details, and manage seller ratings to ensure only verified sellers can list books.

**Order Oversight** – Monitor all orders across the platform, view sales metrics, and intervene if an order or seller needs attention.

**Logout** – Securely log out from the BookStore app.
